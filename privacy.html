Neend Ka Nishana — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

No account is required to play. Signing in with Google is optional and only needed to submit scores. Analytics is off until you allow it.

Who we are

Neend Ka Nishana is developed by Sarka ("we", "us"). For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact privacy@neendkanishana.com.

Guest play

You can play fully offline as a guest. Best scores, stars, unlocked levels, settings and the 18+ acknowledgement stay on your device. We do not sell data, show ads, or use advertising identifiers.

Google sign-in and leaderboard (optional)

If you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication processes your Google account ID, display name, email address and profile photo. Your display name, photo and score are shown publicly on the leaderboard. Your email is not shown publicly and is not shared with other players. Guests can keep local scores without signing in.

Gameplay analytics (optional)

If you allow gameplay analytics, Firebase Analytics records events such as mode selected, run start/end, score, duration, hits, misses and result. We do not send your name, email, Google account ID, contacts, location or advertising ID to Analytics. Analytics is off until you opt in and can be turned off in Settings at any time.

Permissions

The app requests Internet access for Google sign-in, the leaderboard and analytics. It does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS or storage permissions.

Sharing and processors

We do not sell or rent personal data. Google/Firebase (Google Cloud) processes authentication, leaderboard and analytics data on our behalf under Google's terms. We may disclose data if required by law.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you use Settings → Reset progress or uninstall the app. Requesting account deletion removes you from the leaderboard immediately and purges your account and remaining server data after 30 days. Sign in again within 30 days to cancel. You can also email privacy@neendkanishana.com.

Children and age rating

The game is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Local data is app-private. Online traffic uses HTTPS/TLS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes

We may update this policy when features change. The updated date will change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
