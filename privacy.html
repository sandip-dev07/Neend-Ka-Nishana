Neend Ka Nishana — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

No account is required. The game works offline. Scores, stars, settings and the optional display name are stored only on your device. We do not sell data, show ads, or use advertising identifiers.

Who we are

Neend Ka Nishana is developed by Sarka ("we", "us"). For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact privacy@neendkanishana.com.

Data stored on your device

Best scores, campaign stars, unlocked levels, daily scores, the leaderboard display name, muted/haptics settings and the 18+ acknowledgement are stored in the app's private storage. They are not transmitted unless the online leaderboard is enabled.

Online leaderboard (optional)

The current build uses a local leaderboard. If a Firebase-backed leaderboard is enabled in a future build, the following may be uploaded: your chosen display name, score, timestamp and an anonymous Firebase user ID. This is used only to show the leaderboard. We do not collect email, phone, contacts, location, photos, camera, microphone or advertising identifiers.

Permissions

The app requests Internet access for the optional online leaderboard. It does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS or storage permissions.

Sharing and processors

We do not sell or rent personal data. If the online leaderboard is enabled, Firebase (Google Cloud) processes the leaderboard records on our behalf under Google's terms. We may disclose data if required by law.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you use Settings → Reset progress or uninstall the app. Online leaderboard records can be deleted by emailing privacy@neendkanishana.com with your display name; we aim to respond within 30 days.

Children and age rating

The game is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Local data is kept in app-private storage. Online leaderboard traffic uses HTTPS/TLS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes

We may update this policy when features change. The updated date at the top of this page will change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
